## AI-Search (Google SGE / ChatGPT) Positioning Strategy

Modern AI search favors content with:
1. **First-hand experience signals** — Nobevra should publish real data from platform aggregates (average invoice payment time, DSO benchmarks) as original research.
2. **Non-commodity information** — Each guide must include at least one piece of original analysis, calculation tool, or proprietary insight not available on competitor pages.
3. **Helpful, specific answers to specific questions** — The HowTo and FAQPage schemas directly feed AI search answer generation.
4. **E-E-A-T signals** — About page (`/free-invoice-maker-app-about`), pricing transparency (`/pricing`), legal docs (`/compliance`, `/dpa`, `/esa`), and security (`/security`) collectively establish trustworthiness.
